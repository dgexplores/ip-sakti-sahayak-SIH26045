# Patents Act, 1970 — Key provisions for Ayurveda

## Sec 3(p) — Not patentable (TK bar)
An invention which, in effect, is traditional knowledge or which is an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known component or components is not an invention within the meaning of this Act.

> Sec 3(p): "an invention which, in effect, is traditional knowledge or which is an aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally known component or components." — Patents Act, 1970.

## Sec 3(d) — mere discovery
Mere discovery of a new form/property of known substance without enhanced efficacy — not patentable.

## Sec 10 — Complete specification
Requires best method, deposit of biological material under Budapest Treaty if not otherwise available.

## Sec 25 — Opposition (incl. TK ground)
TKDL-backed opposition under Sec 25(1)(k) and 25(2)(k).
