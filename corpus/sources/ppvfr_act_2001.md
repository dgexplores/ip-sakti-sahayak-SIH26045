# PPV&FR Act, 2001

Protects new/extant plant varieties via PPV&FR Authority.
- Breeder, farmer, extant variety categories.
- Ayurveda: new high-yield medicinal cultivar (e.g., high-withanolide ashwagandha) → PPV&FR, not patent (Sec 3(j) exclusion for plants).
Link: https://plantauthority.gov.in/ppv-fr-act
