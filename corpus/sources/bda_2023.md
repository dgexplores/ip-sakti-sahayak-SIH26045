# Biological Diversity Act, 2023 + Rules 2024

## Sec 3 — Access approval (foreign entities)
Foreign person/entity accessing Indian biological resource/TK for research/commercial utilization → prior approval of NBA.

## Sec 7 — Intimation (Indian entities)
Indian persons/entities → prior intimation to SBB before accessing for commercial utilization; BDA 2023 retains tiered approach.

> Span: "No person who is a citizen of India or body corporate... shall obtain any biological resource... for commercial utilization... without giving prior intimation to the State Biodiversity Board." — Sec 7 (as amended 2023).

## Benefit-sharing (Sec 21, 6)
NBA determines benefit-sharing on mutually agreed terms (MAT), includes monetary + non-monetary.

## Rule 2024
SBB forms, timelines, offenses/penalties streamlined. Non-compliance → imprison/fine.
