# Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999

Protects names indicating goods originating in a territory with quality/reputation due to geography (e.g., Darjeeling Tea, Mysore Sandal).
- Register at GI Registry, Chennai.
- Owner: association of persons/producers; authorized users licensed.
- Ayurveda angle: region-specific formulations (e.g., Kerala Ayurveda oils) may qualify; GI is collective, not individual patent.
