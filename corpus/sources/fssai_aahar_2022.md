# FSSAI Ayurveda Aahar Regulations, 2022

## Reg 3(1)
Ayurveda Aahar means food recipes described in authoritative Ayurveda texts (Schedule B list).

## Boundary
- If consumed as food with health claim → FSSAI Aahar, not AYUSH drug.
- Claims restricted by Food Safety Act + Magic Remedies Act (no cure-all claims).
