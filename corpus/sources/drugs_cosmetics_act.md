# Drugs & Cosmetics Act — AYUSH pathways

- Classical (First Schedule): license via State SLA, GMP (Schedule T), no clinical trial.
- Proprietary Ayurveda (Sec 3(h) + 2024 notification): need safety/efficacy dossier, sometimes pilot study.
- Phytopharmaceutical (Schedule Y, CDSCO): purified, standardized fraction ≥4 bioactive markers, full NDA-like dossier.
- New Drug: full clinical trial.
Decision hinges on formulation classification flow.
