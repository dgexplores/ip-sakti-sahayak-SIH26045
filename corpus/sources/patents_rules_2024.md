# Patents (Amendment) Rules, 2024 — Diffs

Effective 15 Mar 2024. Key changes vs 2003:
- Timeline compressed: RQ filing 31 months (PCT national phase), FER response 3 months.
- Grace period and working statement (Form 27) frequency reduced to once in 3 years.
- Pre-grant representation handling streamlined.
- Relevant to Ayurveda: faster prosecution helps perishable market windows; dosha-specific claims need careful Form 2 drafting.
